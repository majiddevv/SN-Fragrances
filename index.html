About SN Fragrances

SN Fragrances is a Karachi perfume house owned by Saad Naeem. We make long-lasting perfumes and attars for men and women. Every scent is blended to last through a full day, from fresh everyday perfumes to rich ouds for evenings and events.

How to choose your perfume

Start with the scent family you already like. Fresh scents feel clean and light, sweet scents are warm and cosy, woody scents are calm and grown-up, floral scents are soft and romantic, and oud is deep and smoky. If you're not sure, a sample set lets you wear five scents for a few days each before you choose a full bottle.

Perfume or attar?

Our perfumes are Eau de Parfum sprays. Attars are concentrated, alcohol-free oils in a roll-on bottle, so a small amount lasts a long time and they're easy to carry.

Make your scent last longer

  • Spray on your wrists, neck and behind your ears, where your skin is warm.
  • Apply after a shower, on moisturised skin.
  • Don't rub your wrists together after spraying.
  • Keep bottles in a cool, dark place, away from sunlight and heat.
